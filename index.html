The short version

Three ideas worth remembering

  1. A calorie is a unit of energy

    One food calorie is the energy needed to raise a kilogram of water by one degree. Everything you eat is fuel your body can measure — and it does, all day, every day.

  2. Balance is what drives change

    Eat more than you burn and you store the surplus. Eat less and you draw on stored energy. That's the entire engine — hormones, appetite and timing all work through it.

  3. Numbers are a tool, not a verdict

    Tracking calories works because it replaces guesswork with information. Used lightly, it's a compass. Used harshly, it stops being useful. The math is on your side either way.
